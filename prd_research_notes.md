# PRD Research Notes

## E-signature workflow guardrails

The Attorney-General’s Department states that the Electronic Transactions Act does not prescribe a single signing method. The selected method must identify the signer, indicate the signer’s intent to approve the communication, be connected to the electronic communication, and be as reliable as appropriate in the circumstances (or proven accurate). The department also notes that consent to electronic signing may be needed in some non-government contexts. The PRD should therefore require an explicit consent/intent statement, signer identification inputs, an evidence record, a tamper-evident final document, and a customer-accessible copy. Source: https://www.ag.gov.au/legal-system/electronic-signatures-documents-and-transactions/electronic-signatures

## Variation workflow guardrails

NSW Government guidance for residential building contracts states that variations must be written, attached to the contract, and signed by both parties or their nominated representatives. It indicates that variation records should describe the work, show cost implications and price calculations, and state any additional time. A limited urgency exception may apply where there is danger to people or property. The product should provide jurisdiction-aware prompts and labels, calculate before/after value and schedule impact, collect approval before non-urgent work starts, and retain an immutable evidence trail. Source: https://www.nsw.gov.au/housing-and-construction/building-or-renovating-a-home/preparing/contracts

## GST and customer-ready document guardrails

The Australian Taxation Office states that GST is generally 10% for sales connected with Australia. Tax invoice requirements differ from a quotation and depend on circumstances. The product should label customer-ready estimates as quotes by default, allow an editable GST rate, and avoid presenting a quote as a tax invoice. Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/international-tax-for-business/gst-for-non-resident-businesses/how-australian-gst-works and https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/tax-invoices
